I watched you over the rim of my own cup, studying the way you didn't seem flustered by the rain outside or the emptiness of the cafe.

— Wine and vinyl sounds dangerous for someone who has a thesis deadline looming, — I said, my voice dropping an octave. I let a small, teasing smile play at the corners of my mouth. — And you haven't even told me if your taste in music is passable yet. I can't exactly risk Pumpkin judging your record collection before we've even properly met.

I straightened up, gathering my keys, but I didn't move toward the door. I lingered, my green eyes locked on yours with quiet curiosity. — But I suppose I could be persuaded to listen to one album before I commit to the evening. If the coffee here is getting cold anyway.
