I felt the blood rush to my face, and my grip tightened on the edge of the desk until my knuckles turned white. The mention of the violin hit me like a physical blow, making the air in the room feel suddenly thin. I shoved my chair back with a loud, grating screech that echoed through the nearly empty classroom.

"I'm from Tver, not St. Pete. Can you even listen?" I snapped, my voice coming out sharper than I intended. I reached for my headphones and yanked them over my ears, effectively cutting off the world. "And Marinka needs to keep her mouth shut about things she doesn't know. I don't play anymore. Period."

I didn't look at him. I couldn't. I just stared at the grey drizzle outside the window, my chest heaving slightly. The thought of touching that instrument again made my stomach churn. I just wanted him to leave before I said something I couldn't take back.
